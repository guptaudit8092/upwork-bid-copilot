# Follow-ups and Weekly Review

## Interview follow-up (send within a few hours)

Draft for the user after every interview:

```
Hi [Name],

Thanks for the time today. Quick recap of what I heard:
- [Their main goal]
- [Key constraint or priority]
- [What success looks like to them]

Here's how I'd use the first week:
1. [Day 1–2 action]
2. [Day 3–4 action]
3. [End-of-week deliverable]

One question to make sure I start in the right place: [specific clarifying question]?

[User's name]
```

Keep it under ~150 words, specific to the conversation, no generic praise.

## Weekly proposal performance review

Read-only analysis. Never submit, withdraw or edit anything.

1. Pull proposals from the last 7 days (plus 30 days for trend).
2. For each: job, client quality, proposal count at time of applying, rate, boosted?, viewed?, replied?, interviewed?, hired?
3. Compare view/reply rates across:
   - boosted vs. not boosted
   - early (< 5 proposals) vs. late
   - rate bands (below floor / at floor / above)
   - strong vs. weak clients
4. Report Connects spent this week and current balance.
5. Give 3–5 concrete, data-backed adjustments for next week. Say plainly when the sample is too small to conclude anything.

### Report format

```
Week of [dates]
Proposals: [n] · Viewed: [n] ([%]) · Replies: [n] · Interviews: [n] · Hires: [n]
Connects: [spent] spent · [balance] left

What worked: …
What didn't: …
Next week:
1. …
2. …
3. …
```
